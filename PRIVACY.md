# TSX Gym Booker – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 September 2026_

TSX Gym Booker is a Chrome extension that fills and submits the TSX gym
booking Google Form on your behalf.

## What is stored

The extension stores the following in your browser only, using
`chrome.storage.local`:

- the name, company email, company name and contact number you enter in
  **Settings**;
- the booking form URL and field IDs (defaults are bundled with the extension);
- the queue of pending bookings and a short history of days you have booked,
  used to colour the calendar and update the toolbar icon.

## How it is used

Your details are used for one purpose: to fill in the TSX gym booking Google
Form when you click **Book**. They are sent only to that form, in the same way
as if you had typed them in yourself.

## What is not done

- No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
- No analytics, tracking or advertising is used.
- The extension does not read or modify any website other than the booking
  form (`https://docs.google.com/forms/*`).
- No code is downloaded or executed from the network; all code is packaged
  with the extension.

## Deleting your data

Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored. You can also
clear or change your details at any time in **Settings**.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the repository that hosts this
extension.
