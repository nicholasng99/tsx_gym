# TSX Gym Booker privacy policy

_Last updated: 22 September 2026_

TSX Gym Booker is a Chrome extension that fills and submits the TSX gym
booking Google Form on your behalf.

## What is stored

The extension stores the following in your browser only, using
`chrome.storage.local`.

- The name, company email, company name and contact number you enter in
  **Settings**.
- The booking form URL and field IDs. The defaults ship with the extension.
- The queue of pending bookings and a short history of days you have booked.
  The extension uses these to colour the calendar and update the toolbar icon.

## How it is used

The extension uses your details for one purpose, which is to fill in the TSX
gym booking Google Form when you click **Book**. It sends them only to that
form, the same as if you had typed them in yourself.

## What is not done

- The extension sends no data to the developer or to any third party.
- It has no analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It does not read or modify any website other than the booking form
  (`https://docs.google.com/forms/*`).
- It does not download or run code from the network. All code is packaged
  with the extension.

## Deleting your data

Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored. You can also
clear or change your details at any time in **Settings**.

## Contact

You can raise questions about this policy in the repository that hosts this
extension.
